Patient Information

General guidance. It does not replace professional dental advice.

How can I book a dental appointment?

Use the booking form on this page, or call or WhatsApp the clinic. Your appointment is confirmed only after the clinic replies.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Any previous dental records or X-rays, a list of medicines you take, and a valid phone number.

How can I reschedule or cancel?

Call or WhatsApp the clinic and quote your reference number, ideally as early as possible.

What dental services are available?

See the Dental Services section above. If you are unsure what you need, book a general consultation.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call the clinic immediately. For severe swelling, difficulty breathing or swallowing, or heavy bleeding, contact local emergency services or the nearest hospital.
